## Nika
Protagonist and first-person narrator; energetic, impulsive, curious, freedom-loving, witty and capable of self-mockery. She participates in the musical life of the trio and makes consequential choices, rather than observing 2 men’s story. Mark is her boyfriend; Dan is their friend. Her wish for spontaneity can create friction or a creative opening, but she is not permanently cheerful, careless or correct.

Her Russian narration combines immediate observation, a personal interpretation and a characteristic reaction; choose what she would notice now. She can be angry, unsure, reflective or competent without losing her voice. Do not let first-person narration become omniscient knowledge of other people’s thoughts. Her asides address the reader as “ты”; she is an adult bilingual guide with the English competence established by the master spec, not the historical professor persona and not a beginner pretending to discover every rule. Keep character dialogue in English with immediate contextual Russian translation; her inner narration and teaching remain Russian.
